# QINEX KENYA (Mental Health Connect)

## Overview

QINEX KENYA is a modern digital mental health platform designed to connect users in Kenya with **licensed psychologists, counsellors, and mental health professionals** instantly. The platform removes traditional barriers such as long waiting times, lack of access, and stigma by offering **private, secure, and on-demand mental health support**.

Users can access professional help through chat, audio, or video sessions at any time, from any device.
